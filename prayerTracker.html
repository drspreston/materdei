The 20-Minute Method

Three movements of Catholic contemplative prayer

01 • LECTIO (00:00 – 05:00) Scripture
Read the day's sacred passage slowly three times. Listen attentively for a single word or phrase that resonates deeply in your spirit.
02 • MEDITATIO (05:00 – 15:00) Silent Contemplation
Close your eyes in silence. Ponder the scripture and the day's Meditatio prompt. Converse quietly with Jesus heart-to-heart.
03 • ORATIO & CONTEMPLATIO (15:00 – 20:00) Thanksgiving & Fruit
Offer praises, intercede for family, and jot down the spiritual insights or peace received in the Grace Note field.
